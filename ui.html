Silent Ear

    Summary

    Voices

    When you give a speaker a name, their voice is saved here, and later recordings label it with that name from the start. A voice is saved from the transcript's recording, so “Save audio” must be on. Voice prints stay on this computer, in voices.json.

      Devices

      What a recording captures. The meters show what each device delivers right now: speak, or play something, and the bar should move.

      Dictionary

      The preferred spellings are given to the model as a hint before each chunk. Anything listed under “heard as” is then always replaced in the text, whole words only, ignoring case. Separate several variants with commas.
